## Guiding constraints

1. Trust is the product; AI is not the marketing.
2. Never fabricate vehicle history, market sizes or provider access.
3. Marketplace MVP → evidence-based verification → revenue validation → AI scale.
4. One platform, separate tenant dashboards, backend-enforced isolation.
5. Khmer and English from day one, mobile-first and low-bandwidth.
6. No customer funds held; no escrow or marketplace split payments without separate legal/provider review.
